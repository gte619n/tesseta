How it works

One source of truth, everywhere you look.

01

The backend owns the record

A single service holds the only connection to your data store and normalizes every signal once. Your phone, watch, and browser are readers of that record — never independent copies drifting apart.

02

In sync across devices

Log a set on your phone and it's there on the web. Money-path writes are atomic, so concurrent edits never lose or double-count. The goal is simple: never lose your data.

03

Works when the network doesn't

The Android app is offline-first: reads come from an encrypted on-device mirror and writes are journaled and replayed when you're back online. Finish the workout in the basement gym; it syncs later.

Privacy & trust

Health data, held to a health-data standard.

This is PHI-grade information, and it's treated that way. Access is authenticated and denied by default. Data is strictly scoped per user — you can only ever reach your own records.

  • Authenticated access only, with Google sign-in across web, phone, and watch
  • Strict per-user scoping — no cross-account access, by construction
  • Secrets envelope-encrypted at rest with managed keys (KMS); TLS in transit
  • The on-device mirror is encrypted with SQLCipher

The principle

Your record is yours. The system is built so that the easy path and the private path are the same path.
